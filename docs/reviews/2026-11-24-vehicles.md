# Review: Phase 4.0 vehicles and validity dates

- **Branch reviewed:** `feat/backend-vehicles` (c8cdbb3, fixes in e8df89e)
- **Reviewer:** independent review agent (Opus 5.5), read-only. It probed a copy of the code.
- **Integration check:** Sonnet 5.5, over real HTTP
- **Date:** 2026-11-24

## Scope

- **Vehicles:**
  - A normalised, unique registration number.
  - Encrypted chassis and engine numbers, masked unless revealed.
  - A responsible employee.
  - Phone and challan status, each with a source.
- **Validity dates:** insurance, PUCC, fitness, tax and permit, each with history.
- **Routes:** `/vehicles`.
- **Jobs** can link a vehicle.
- **Customer search** also matches registration numbers.
- **Migration:** vehicles, vehicle_expiries and jobs.vehicle_id.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Finding | Outcome |

| L2 | Low | A transfer left open jobs pointing at another customer's vehicle. | **Fixed.** A vehicle can't be transferred while open jobs use it. |
| L3 | Low | Transfer audits held field names only. | **Fixed.** They also record from/to ids. |
| L4 | Low | Clearing an expiry that was never set created a history row. | **Fixed.** It records nothing now. Edits to inactive vehicles remain allowed; owner decision. |

## Integration check (real HTTP)

All seven steps passed:

1. Setup.
2. Creating a vehicle:
   - The response is masked.
   - A duplicate returns 409.
   - Bad registration and chassis values return 422.
3. Access:
   - The responsible employee can reveal the numbers. A colleague on an open job sees them masked and gets 403 for reveal and for chassis edits. Someone without access gets 404.
   - The audit has no identifier values.
4. Expiries:
   - Insurance history is kept, newest first.
   - `days_left` is correct in Asia/Kolkata time, including negative for expired.
   - Clearing a type that was never set records nothing.
5. Jobs:
   - `vehicle` appears on the job and in the list.
   - Another customer's vehicle can't be linked.
   - A transfer is blocked while a job is open, and allowed and audited once it closes.
6. Search scope:
   - Admin sees all; the responsible employee sees their own; an employee without access sees nothing.
7. The contract change is additive.

## Checked and found sound

- **Encryption:** the encryption context is per vehicle and part. Lists and PATCH responses are always masked.
- **Access:** hidden vehicles give 404 everywhere.
- **Expiry documents:** they must belong to the same customer and be accessible.
- **Locking:** the row lock prevents races on the "current" index, and the lock order doesn't conflict with jobs.
- **Queries:** no N+1 queries.
- **Migration:** the round trip works and `alembic check` is clean.
- **Schemas:** no schema-name clashes.

## Checks

- **Suite:** `scripts/check.sh backend` gives 609 passed, with the contract in sync on the owner branch.
- **Deliberate breaks:** 17 of 18 caught. The missed one was removing the duplicate-registration pre-check, which the database unique constraint still enforces with the same 409.
